DejaVuSans.ttf and DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf in this folder are the DejaVu fonts (copied unchanged from
vendor/dompdf/dompdf/lib/fonts). They are used server-side by App\Actions\Publications\GenerateFlyer
to render the Humas e-flyer PNG.

DejaVu fonts are derived from the Bitstream Vera fonts and are distributed under a free license
(Bitstream Vera license + public-domain DejaVu changes; Arev additions under the same terms).
Full license text: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html
