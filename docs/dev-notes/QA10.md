## Security (login, reset, 2FA)
1. **Anyone who knew a student's NIS could keep the account locked** (K3 forbids it): the per-account hourly limit
   counted every request, correct passwords included, and the progressive lock was per account and refreshed its own
   counter; "Lupa kata sandi" did not clear it. Now: the progressive lock and the `throttle:login` limits are per account
   **and** client address (an IPv6 address by its /64) and the hourly one counts refused passwords only (`login-h` 20/h
   via `Limit::after()` + `AuthenticateUser::failed()`); a successful login clears its own address;
   `LoginLockout::clear()` starts a new "generation" of the account, so a reset by code, an Admin reset, "Buka ulang" or an
   unfreeze empties every lock and limit of that account, its TOTP failures and its OTP limits. The message is unchanged.
   *Review follow-up (D-79):* locks per address alone left no ceiling per account for a guessable initial password tried
   from many addresses. 30 failures within 24 h from every address now close the account to devices that never signed in
   to it until those 24 h end; a device that did (`KnownDevices`, cookie `sth_device`: encrypted, httpOnly, 180 days,
   bound to the account and its current password hash, at most 8 accounts per browser) still gets in, and the Admins
   (plus Kesiswaan for a student account) get the alert `account.login_attack` once per window (`LoginAttackAlert`). The
   total still raises `security.login_failures_account` (5, 10, 20, then every 20).
   *Second review:* a stranger at the owner's address (school NAT) still held the owner's known device: the address lock
   and the per-pair limits are now split by device kind (`pair()` suffix `|k`). An identifier sent as a list gave a 500
   (now read as empty). The pair lock is written before the alert, which is failure-safe. At most 5 attack alerts an hour
   (both kinds), the rest only logged (`security.login_attack_alerts_capped`). A browser stays known after the owner
   changes the password while signed in.
   **The retry time jumped** (the lead's report): two clocks answered for one account and address, the per-minute route
   limit (which also counted the tries made during a lock) and the progressive lock, so pressing "Masuk" during a
   15-minute lock showed "14 menit", "45 detik", "13 menit", and after the 45 seconds the login was still refused.
   `LoginLockout::secondsUntilAllowed()` now gives the longest of the address lock, the account lock for unknown devices
   and every full route limit; the named limiter answers every block with it before counting, so a refused try counts
   only towards the per-address `login-ip` limit (bounds floods) and the time only goes down (up only when a new, longer
   step starts). Unknown identifiers get the same times (`LoginRetryTimeQa10Test`). Checked on the running local stack
   over HTTP with a fictional identifier: 55 → 45 → 30 → 15 → 4 detik during the 1-minute lock, then a steady
   "15 menit" / "14 menit" under nine quick tries during the 15-minute lock.
2. **The reset code could be brute-forced**: the "Lupa kata sandi" limits were keyed on the TYPED identifier (NIS, NISN,
   email and accent variants of one account each had their own counter). The first fix (limits per account) let a
   stranger who knows a NIS use up the owner's codes and wrong-code allowance (review). Now the screen limits are per
   resolved account **and** address (`ResetCodeLimits`: ask 3 per 15 min and 10 a day, check 10 per 10 min, plus 30 per
   address); a reset code belongs to the browser that asked for it (`OtpRequester`: a random session token, the code ids
   in the cache for 25 h), so only that browser can use it and only its own new code cancels its older one; per browser
   the cooldown, 5 codes an hour, 10 a day and 10 wrong codes a day (`security.otp_daily_limit`); per account at most 20
   reset codes a day (≤ 100 guesses, second review) and an Admin alert `account.reset_code_attack` once a day when the
   wrong reset codes of all browsers reach 20 (no block). Codes for signed-in users (activation, email change) keep the
   per account + purpose limits. After an Admin reset or an unfreeze the OTP limits count only codes made after
   `LoginLockout::clearedAt()` and the screen limits are released (keyed under the account's generation); code requests
   per address are 30 an hour as in the §3b table (were 30 per 15 minutes). The activation screen names the minutes to
   wait.
3. **Staff TOTP challenge**: the limiter was keyed per account+address (more addresses, more guesses) and a challenge
   survived a password change. Now keyed on the account; `TwoFactorChallenge` + `GuardedTwoFactorLoginRequest` keep the
   challenge start time and a keyed fingerprint of the password hash and refuse a challenge older than 10 minutes, after
   a password change, for an account that cannot log in, or after 10 wrong codes in an hour
   (`security.two_factor_challenge_ended`); every wrong code is audited (`two_factor.challenge_failed`, label "Kode 2FA
   salah saat masuk"). The wrong-code counter sits under the account's lock generation, so `clear()` resets it.
4. **A new Admin / TU / Kesiswaan account without its role yet** (D-61) chose its password under the student rules:
   `SetNewPassword` now asks "is this a student account", not "has a staff role".

## Data, privacy, notices
5. **Auto-publish published a disabled or deleted student** at the scheduled time and mailed every Humas: the "named"
   rule (consent, not deleted, not disabled; alumni allowed) is `App\Actions\Publications\NamedStudent`, used by
   `PublicationPolicy::eligible()`, the auto-publish job and the Humas queues.

   named and is not sent when there are none.

    notices (guru role + can log in) everywhere.
11. **Kepala Sekolah told to cancel in an Admin-only menu**, and not told how a delayed role change or a self-cancelled
    replacement ended: wording per reader and the recipients of ARCHITECTURE §8b.

    revisi" with "Ketua tim memperbaiki laporan dari akunnya." and "Lihat lomba".
13. **Toasts and lines that claimed notices** (D-109): Kepala Sekolah reject toast and the decided row ("Ditolak ·

## Registrations and student screens
14. **A lomba closed early stranded team invitations**: `CloseCompetition` says existing registrations keep running,
    but confirm / invite / withdraw required a published lomba ("Batas pendaftaran sudah lewat." before the deadline).
    `RegistrationPolicy::teamWindowOpen()`: an existing registration stays open until `registration_closes_at`; a new
    one still needs an open lomba; "Ikut tim" shows only while it works.
15. **An expired invitation read "Kamu sudah menolak"**: recognised from the audit row `registration.invitations_expired`
    → "Undangan ini sudah tidak berlaku karena batas pendaftaran lewat." / chip "Tidak menjawab".
16. **Registration failed with no message** when the lomba's default guru pembimbing was disabled: the form pre-fills
    only an offered guru and shows the Action's refusal on the field.
17. **A rejected report showed "Poin & apresiasi" as done**: the tracker ends at "Laporan hasil" with "Laporan ditolak,
    tanpa poin"; Riwayat shows "Laporan ditolak".
18. Counts of unanswered invitations as "Lomba diikuti" (Portofolio, PDF, Profil talenta), "N siswa SMAGA sudah
    mendaftar", and the guru's binaan: confirmed members only.
19. An empty page after "Tandai dibaca" on the last page of Notifikasi, or a stale `?page=` on Cari lomba: the last page
    with rows. Cari lomba order follows its comment (open, upcoming, closed newest first). Detail lomba of a proposal
    that was closed or rejected no longer shows a green "Dibuka" chip.

## Lomba, points, exports
20. **Kepala Sekolah › Laporan PDF had no row limit** (a whole school's results ran dompdf out of memory in the web
    request; measured: 400 rows > 256 MB): at most 250 rows (`ExportPrestasiReport::MAX_PDF_ROWS`); above it the button
    is disabled with a note to use Excel or narrow the period. The year list is one small query.
21. Switching a lomba to individu or lowering "Maks anggota" while a team registration runs is refused.
22. A disabled default guru pembimbing blocked every save of a lomba: the unchanged one is accepted and named "(tidak
    aktif)". A cleared date field was compared with "now": blank = null. Uploads of a failed save are discarded.
23. Admin › Bobot could not be saved again once a tingkat weight was 1000 ("1.000" read as 1 with three decimals).
24. Dashboard top five numbered ties 1–5: it uses the public `Ranker`. "N laporan lewat batas" counted revision requests:
    only registrations without a report.
25. `NoWebAddress` missed short links and country domains (shorturl.at, rb.gy, youtu.be, .ru …); ordinary names such as
    "O2SN 2026", "FLS3N Tk. Provinsi", "S.Pd." stay allowed (tests on both sides).
26. Scheduled commands: `--date` is refused in production by `sth:registrations:advance` / `sth:reports:remind` (like
    `sth:arsip-otomatis`); every job's overlap lock lasts an hour, so a killed run no longer skips the next day.

## Requests for the lead
- **Re-invite after expiry**: a member whose invitation expired cannot be invited again after the deadline was extended
  (D-50 records an expiry as a decline; the search chip still says "Sudah menolak").
- **Late from the start**: a registration that reaches "menunggu laporan" after `final_on` + N days (late approval or
  letters) is late from its first day; count the deadline from when reporting became possible?
- **Laporan PDF cap 250** (*sementara*-style): a whole school year now comes as Excel or a PDF per bidang; the other
  choice is a PDF with the summary and the first 250 rows.
- **Team guard scope**: today any running team blocks "individu" and a lower "Maks anggota"; refuse only when a team is
  already bigger than the new limit?
- **Login trade-offs**: one person on one address can reach the 30-failure account lock in about 10 h; the owner on a
  new device then waits until the 24 h end or uses "Lupa kata sandi" / an Admin reset (known devices are not affected);
  with 2 addresses the same person can also use up the account's 20 reset codes a day, and then only an Admin reset
  helps while the screen still says "kode telah dikirim" (the real fix: a longer code or a signed link); a school PC
  where the victim once signed in is a known device for anyone at it (own quota) until the victim changes the password;
  one flooding client at the school NAT makes every account there wait up to 60 s; past 5 attack alerts an hour the
  Admins see nothing more that hour; the code-to-browser link lives in the cache (a column on `otp_codes` would be
  sturdier, migration); a classmate behind the school NAT can still slow the owner's new device on that address; someone
  with a staff password can block that TOTP step for an hour; a right password on a frozen account clears that
  address's counter before the freeze refusal (weak oracle); a few timing differences sit outside the Timebox. NIS /
  NISN / email share one bucket, which can tell whether an email is a student's bound address.
- **Other**: letter number uniqueness without case AND spaces needs a `number_key` column (migration; race unlikely);
  moving `final_on` later after "menunggu laporan"; `VerifyReport` reads the active weight version outside its
  transaction; gurus with a pending activation are offered as pembimbing; Humas › Arsip and "Tambah prestasi" still
  use consent only (not the named rule); the "named" rule lives in three places.
